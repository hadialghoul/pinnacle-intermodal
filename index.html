History of Our Creation

At Pinnacle Intermodal, we are committed to delivering excellence in every mile. As a trusted transportation and logistics partner, we specialize in intermodal trucking, container movement, and reliable freight solutions across the United States. With years of industry experience, we have built a reputation for on-time delivery, safe cargo handling, and unmatched customer service.

Our operations are supported by a strong regional presence, with established offices in Toledo, Ohio and Detroit, Michigan, allowing us to efficiently serve major ports, rail terminals, and distribution centers throughout the Midwest. To meet the growing demand for dependable transportation services, we are excited to announce that a new office in Columbus, Ohio is coming soon — expanding our reach and enhancing our ability to support more clients across the region.
